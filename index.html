Intelligent blocking that keeps you on task.

Whip is a focus guard for Mac and iPhone. Tell it what you're working on in one line and it keeps you on track, with no blocklists to maintain. Sites that can go either way, like YouTube and Reddit, are judged by the content you're actually looking at.

You keep every resource that helps, and Whip takes care of the blocking with almost no input from you.
